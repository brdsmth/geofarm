The four clarifying amendments REVIEW-003 §6 proposed, adopted through the normal open process (RFC-0006 Amendment 2, RFC-0015 Amendment 1, RFC-0000 Amendment 2), each with its code:

- **A1** — viewport and View scope are one value (normative); trail granularity: `ViewTrail.slide()` coalesces continuous navigation, verbs punctuate.
- **A2** — density-triggered aggregation: the count badge at N ≥ 2, and the aggregate resolves — a stacked dot lists its members.
- **A3** — inspection honors the temporal binding or declares its frame (landed at P-49; now stated in the RFC).
- **A4** — the S10 lint enforces location as well as vocabulary: user-facing copy outside the surface package fails, named deviations are marked in place and counted; `index.html` carries structure only.

The P1 backlog (REVIEW-003 §8) landed with it: crop-at-a-glance colouring from projected state; year ticks under the season slider; every string relocated to the surface package; search results with place context and keyboard navigation; zoom controls and a scale bar; the whole View remembered (camera, season, lenses, selection), per viewer. From P2: the farm's own pulse (this season on the farm, in the farm line's story), pick disambiguation, a small colour system for marks, glove-scale targets.

The shell also grew its M6/M7 faces: **Looking as** (switch viewer; the same map, a different sub-world), **Who can see this?** with sharing as one decision (who, what kind of thing, until when — or what is inside a drawn line) and **Stop sharing**, the outbox durable in Web Storage per viewer, the world itself persisted on the device so a note written today is there tomorrow.
